# M08 formatting scope and authority

Target: BMC Genomics, Research article, pre-submission content-review copy. Checked 16 October 2026. This is a formatting revision of scientific R1, not a new analysis or a submission-ready release.

Official sources:

- [General submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/12864/submission-guidelines): figure titles belong in the manuscript (up to 15 words), legends up to 300 words; panels form one composite. Final PDF widths are 85/170 mm, with a 225 mm maximum for figure plus legend. Figures must remain legible. Main text uses double spacing, line and page numbers, and editable tables.
- [Research article instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/12864/submission-guidelines/research-article): Vancouver examples include six authors followed by “et al.”. This revision consistently uses all authors for lists of six or fewer, otherwise the first six plus “et al.”. This is the selected implementation illustrated by the journal, not a claim that its page explicitly states a unique author cutoff.

Project-specific acceptance: at least 8 pt figure text after reduction to the embedded Word width (590 pixels, approximately 156.1 mm), in addition to a full-width 170 mm proof. This numeric font threshold is our readability criterion, not a quoted journal rule. Overall titles are removed from graphics; panel headings and keys remain. PNG is a listed submission format; SVG is retained as an editable source, not asserted to be an accepted upload format.

All prior R1 text, figures, tables, packages and builders are preserved. Scientific prose, table cells, estimates, uncertainty, denominators, NA and zero solutions remain unchanged. Current work reads saved aggregate figure sources only, never individual CTRP responses or predictions; it performs no fitting, tuning, resampling or scoring. Author declarations, actual asset access, upstream rights and immutable archiving remain pending as previously agreed.
