## A1. Cohort eligibility and identity QC — replace/extend Cohort curation

Cell-line identities were reconciled by authoritative exact CCLE, Broad/DepMap, Sanger and COSMIC cross-references; ambiguous or conflicting identities were not resolved by name similarity. Related models were assigned a common biological group using Broad same-tissue-sample PatientID, Sanger patient, and parent/derivative relationships. Broad PatientID was treated as an identifier for models from the same tissue sample, not as a complete patient genealogy. The cancer-focused cohort-v2 overlay excluded models explicitly annotated as Non-Cancerous and quarantined absent, empty or Unknown disease annotations unless exact-ID cancer provenance was available. One otherwise unannotated model, HCC1588 (ACH-001078; CVCL_A351; SIDM01820), was retained on concordant Cellosaurus, Sanger and KCLB cancer-cell-line evidence. This metadata amendment followed a development-only pilot and preceded external response access by the reconstructed workflow.

The primary CTRPv2 cohort required the published SNP-matched-reference identity-QC status. SNP-unconfirmed and SNP-not-tested models were retained in a separate identity-QC sensitivity list and were not thereby classified as proven misidentifications. The external cohort excluded all known GDSC2 model groups and their known related groups, including candidate identities in unresolved GDSC mappings, rather than only models represented among the eight selected drugs. No additional shared-group reserve was needed. The fixed panel satisfied project feasibility thresholds of at least 200 development and 80 primary external biological groups per compound; these thresholds were not precision or power guarantees. The cohort-v2 overlay retained the original compound identities, panel order and dose windows and yielded 626 development and 175 primary external groups. Tissue labels were canonicalized by exact case-insensitive equivalence to the authoritative vocabulary, without fuzzy or histology-based merging.

Sources: C01–C06. The original cohort-v1 general minimum was 50 external groups with a preferred 80-group pool; all eight were selected from that preferred pool, and cancer cohort-v2 explicitly required 80. Do not rewrite the historical v1 minimum as 80 or imply a newly conducted sensitivity evaluation merely because its identity list exists.

## A2. Gene annotation — extend Data sources

Expression gene records were annotated using the CCLE-matched `gencode.v19.genes.v7_model.patched_contigs.gtf.gz` reference. Annotation was joined on the complete versioned Ensembl gene identifier, not on gene symbol or a version-stripped lookup. Of 57,820 original records, 56,238 had an exact versioned GTF match. Unmatched records remained in the source audit with annotation unresolved; they were not assigned a noncoding biotype. Model inputs were restricted to the 19,836 records explicitly annotated as protein_coding. The terminal numeric Ensembl version suffix was removed to provide stable identifiers for deterministic feature naming and tie-breaking; no genes were collapsed or averaged, and no duplicate unversioned identifiers were found. Original float64 TPM values and their exact ties were retained for rank and pair construction.

Sources: G01–G03. The annotation reference SHA-256 is `225017a79843a51d3428810bc9656e6353dcce570c816acf02ead8f7d23e8956`. The local source audit did not redownload the complete historical TPM to establish byte-for-byte identity to the remote asset; do not claim such verification.

## A3. Repeated development splits — replace seed/split description

Splits were generated by scikit-learn StratifiedGroupKFold with shuffle=True, tissue as the stratification label and biological group as the grouping unit. The 626-group identity table was sorted by canonical model identifier before splitting. Five outer folds were generated for each of three repeats with random states 20261013, 20261014 and 20261015. Within each outer-training partition, three inner folds used random state `repeat_seed + 100 + outer_fold`, with outer_fold indexed from zero. These global assignments were generated once per repeat and outer context, intersected with each compound's eligible records, and shared by all model arms. The resulting folds were checked for group separation and exactly-once validation coverage.

Rare tissue strata could not be represented in every fold. The recorded global split preparation produced three outer and fifteen inner stratification warnings because the least-populated stratum contained one or two groups, fewer than the number of folds. The frozen grouped folds were retained; neither rare tissues nor folds were removed or reassigned according to scores. These split warnings are distinct from the zero recorded elastic-net solver warnings.

Sources: S01–S03. Warning totals summarize captured splitter contexts, not independent model failures. Do not imply fully balanced tissue strata.

## A4. Final fitting and LOTO splits — extend corresponding Methods

For full-development fitting, a shared three-fold StratifiedGroupKFold assignment was generated over the sorted 626-group identity table with random state 2026101501, then intersected with each compound's records. The recorded preparation emitted one rare-stratum warning (one group versus three folds). The same candidate grid and pooled inner-OOF RMSE objective were used, and every inner-training fit rebuilt the learned feature and preprocessing steps.

For LOTO sensitivity, all groups belonging to each held-out tissue were excluded from development for that task. The 23 tissue names were sorted by Python Unicode code-point order and indexed from zero; each tissue's three-fold inner tuning used random state `2026101401 + tissue_index` on the current compound's remaining training groups. All arms shared that drug-by-held-out-tissue split. Across 184 drug-by-tissue contexts, the splitter issued 184 rare-stratum warnings: 176 contexts had a remaining stratum with one group and eight had one with two groups. The three-fold grouped tuning policy was retained with verified group separation and exactly-once validation coverage. LOTO remained descriptive, without a new confidence-interval or testing family.

## A5. FWL prediction for unknown tissues — replace ambiguous zero-effect sentence

For prediction, the fitted model used the training feature scaler and restored the training target mean and standard deviation after adding the tissue and molecular components. An unseen tissue had an all-zero one-hot tissue vector. Thus, a zero standardized tissue contribution did not mean a zero AUC prediction: on the original outcome scale, the prediction was the training overall mean plus any retained molecular contribution. When the molecular coefficients were zero, this reduced to the training overall mean for an unseen tissue, matching the tissue-only reference policy; for an observed tissue, it reduced to that training tissue's mean.

Source: F01. In a full technical account, standardized feature vector z, tissue row d, training tissue-specific feature means M, standardized-target tissue means a, target mean mu_y, target SD s_y and molecular coefficient beta give `prediction = mu_y + s_y * [(z - dM) beta + da]`. For an unseen tissue d=0. This is an implementation identity, not a claim that molecular predictors are unconfounded in a new tissue.

## A6. Definedness, bootstrap and percentile calculation — extend Statistical uncertainty

Development resampling used 10,000 shared group-multiplicity draws over all 626 groups, generated by NumPy Generator(PCG64) with seed 2026101602. Ranks were recomputed as exact tied midranks under each draw's integer multiplicities. The implementation required a total multiplicity of at least two and positive weighted rank variance for both response and prediction; undefined components propagated through paired contrasts, complete-repeat averages and strict eight-drug macros. The development implementation did not impose the separate external rule requiring three distinct observed groups.

The external evaluation used 20,000 shared draws over the 175 external groups with seed 2026101502. A point correlation and each correlation within a draw required at least three distinct represented groups and nonconstant response and prediction. Invalid components retained their reasons; a strict macro draw required every prespecified drug contrast to be defined. An interval was reported only for a defined point with at least 99% valid draws (9,900 of 10,000 development draws or 19,800 of 20,000 external draws). Percentile endpoints used NumPy quantile with method='linear'; family-adjusted endpoints used tail probability 0.05/(2K), with K=9 or 18 for the two separate inferential families. No invalid draw was replaced by additional sampling, no family was shrunk, and no available-case mean replaced a strict macro. For all 27 prespecified development and 27 external estimands, the existing records report all requested draws valid. These are conditional intervals with the previously stated limits, not full retraining uncertainty or equivalence tests.

Sources: T01–T05. LOTO uses a separate descriptive correlation helper: fewer than two held-out samples or constant response yields NA, as does constant prediction. Do not apply the external three-distinct-group rule retrospectively to LOTO or development. The external summary did not originally write the requested exact NumPy/SeedSequence fields; the frozen environment and code identify NumPy 2.3.5 and Generator(PCG64(seed)). A later seed-sequence reconstruction was explicitly recorded as retrospective in stage 14. Do not present it as an original log field or rerun bootstrap to fill it.
